How fast can this phone make a ballot proof?

Every ballot carries a zero-knowledge proof made on the voter's phone. This page makes the same proof three times with test data and measures how long it takes. Nothing about you or your phone is sent anywhere. You copy the result yourself if you want to share it.

What is measured

Proof
π_vote, Groth16 on BN254
Size
32,535 constraints, 4 options
Trees
identity depth 32, revocation depth 32
Prover
snarkjs 0.7.5 in the browser (WebAssembly)
Download
about 24 MB, once

A native prover in a phone app is usually several times faster than a browser. This page gives an upper bound.

Keep the screen on. It can take a minute or more.
