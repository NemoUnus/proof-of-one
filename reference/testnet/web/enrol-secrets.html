Enrolment service secrets (pilot, TEST)

Makes the three secrets of the pilot's enrolment service, 64 random bytes each, with your browser's own random source (crypto.getRandomValues). They are made on this device and sent nowhere: this page has no network code, and you can read its source. Reload to make new ones.

Paste each one into the Worker's Settings → Variables and secrets as a Secret (not as text), under the name shown. Do not save them anywhere else, do not send them to anyone, not even to the project's agents. The service shows only the public keys that belong to them.
