# Provenance — who made what

Proof of One is built by people and by disclosed AI agents ([AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)). This file says, for each part of the project, who wrote it and which humans have reviewed it. It is updated whenever that changes. The goal (Decision Log #28) is for human authorship and human review to grow over time, especially in the parts that protect participants.

## Status labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Human** | Written by a human. |
| **AI draft, human-directed** | Written by AI agents on the instructions of the founder, who took the decisions it records. |
| **AI draft** | Written by AI agents and not yet reviewed by a human. |
| **Founder-reviewed** | Read and approved by the founder. This is not an expert review. |
| **Expert-reviewed** | Reviewed by an independent human expert. The reviewer, under any name, and the scope of the review are listed below. |
