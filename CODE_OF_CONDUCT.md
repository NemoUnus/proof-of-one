# Code of Conduct

Proof of One brings together people from many countries, backgrounds and political views. Our shared goal is a tool that lets every person's voice be counted honestly. We expect conduct that serves that goal.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas, not people.
- Welcome disagreement and criticism of the design; it is how the system becomes safe.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Keep discussions neutral and about the project. Do not use project spaces to campaign for or against any government, party or group.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults or discriminatory language.
- **Attempting to identify, expose or locate any contributor** who participates under a pseudonym, including the founder. This includes speculating about real identities, analysing writing style or activity times for that purpose, and publishing personal information.
- Pressuring anyone to reveal their identity, location or affiliations.
- Deliberately introducing hidden weaknesses or misleading claims.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **NemoUnus@proton.me**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or ban accounts that violate this code. Attempts to de-anonymise contributors lead to a permanent ban.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces — repositories, issues, discussions, chat channels and events — and to public conduct that concerns the project.
