# French glossary (Simenon)

*Fixed terms for every French text of the project: the app, the consent text, poll texts and documents (Decision #68, point 2). One English term always gets the same word. First version: 25 November 2026, made with the same choices as the Persian glossary approved by the founder (for example "encrypted", never "locked"). Changes are made here first, then in the texts.*

**Not yet read by a native speaker.** Before volunteers use this language, at least one native reader reviews its texts (Decision #68, point 5).

## Style

- Short sentences in polite everyday language that an elderly reader can follow; not word-for-word, never administrative.
- Say what the English means, never more: consent and safety texts say no more and no less than the English.
- Placeholders (`%s`), digits and line breaks stay as in the English; dates keep the form the app shows (2026-12-08).
