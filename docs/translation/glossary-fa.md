## Style

- Short sentences; polite everyday Persian («شما»), which an elderly reader can follow.
- Common words whatever their origin; no avoidance of Arabic loanwords (no «سره‌نویسی»).
- Not word-for-word: say what the English means, never more. Consent and safety texts must say no more and no less than the English.
- Placeholders (`%s`), digits and line breaks stay exactly as in the English. Dates stay in the Latin form the app shows (2026-12-08); counts in sentences use Persian digits.
- The «ی» of ezafe after «ه» is written «‌ی» (شماره‌ی), as in the rest of the project.
