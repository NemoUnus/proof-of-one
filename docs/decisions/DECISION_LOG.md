| 30 | **One person, one identity, several verified nationalities.** A member with more than one genuine chip document may add each further document to their existing identity instead of being refused as a duplicate. The enclave checks that the document is genuine, unused, and belongs to the same face, then issues an updated credential with up to three nationality codes and revokes the previous one with the hidden revocation handle. The member may vote once in the regional polls of each of their nationalities. Global polls, like every poll, accept one ballot per person, because the nullifier comes from the identity secret, not from a document. Adding a nationality is optional. The ballot proof shows only that one of the member's nationalities equals the poll's region. Results are never broken down by combination of nationalities. Expiry is kept per document. In the regional budget formula, a member counts in each of their regions, and the number of such members is published. | Founder + team |
| 31 | **Honest reach.** Public texts state plainly how many people can take part today: about one person in six worldwide hold a chip document a phone can read, with large gaps in South Asia and Africa ([coverage research](../research/document-coverage-v0.1.md)). Truthfulness is a principle of the project. | Founder |
| 32 | **Other ID-card standards later.** Enrolment uses ICAO 9303 chip documents only at launch. National ID cards with other chip standards are deferred to regional extensions after the main launch, built openly with developers from the countries concerned and reviewed like every other part. | Founder |
| 33 | **Face uniqueness at scale** ([spec](../specs/face-uniqueness-at-scale-v0.1.md)). (1) Enrolment first asks whether the person has already enrolled with another document; if so, they are guided to add the document (Decision #30). (2) The main duplicate search compares the new face only with members of the same birth date and sex, with a normal threshold. (3) A second search covers all members with a very strict threshold (false-alarm rate in the order of one in a million), so that an issuer printing different birth dates cannot evade detection. (4) Statistics by document issuer reveal abnormal patterns (Decision #21). (5) The stored face template is refreshed whenever a member adds or renews a document. (6) The threshold favours honest people over catching every duplicate, and error rates are published. (7) A person wrongly flagged as a duplicate has a path to a second review. (8) The accuracy study measures errors by time between photos, for phone selfies, and for 1, 10 and 100 million members. Open question: whether a second biometric is needed if face alone proves insufficient. | Founder (points 1, 2 and 5 proposed by the founder) + team |
| 34 | **Access under network restrictions** ([spec](../specs/censorship-resistant-access-v0.1.md)). Using an ordinary VPN or circumvention tool must never break enrolment or voting: no blocking of VPN, Tor or proxy users; circumvention built into the app with several transports; no dependency on Google or Apple servers, accounts, phone numbers or SMS for core functions (hardware key attestation instead); several distribution channels; a signed, updatable endpoint list; tolerance of poor connections; several independent relays; a low-profile presence on the phone. | Founder (raised by the founder) + team |
